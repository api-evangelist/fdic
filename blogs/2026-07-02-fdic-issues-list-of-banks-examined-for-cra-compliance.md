The FDIC released its list of state nonmember banks evaluated for Community Reinvestment Act (CRA) compliance in April 2026. The CRA, enacted in 1977, requires the FDIC to assess banks' records of meeting credit needs in low- and moderate-income communities. Individual CRA evaluations are available from the banks themselves or the FDIC's Public Information Center.
